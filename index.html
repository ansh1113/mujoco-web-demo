How it works

Every physics step the controller computes the whole-body centre of mass c, its velocity v and the capture point ξ = c + v/ω with ω = √(g/z) — where the robot would have to step to come to rest. The error between ξ and the centre of the feet drives an ankle strategy (shift the centre of pressure) and a hip strategy (swing the trunk, keep the pelvis level) as offsets on a slightly crouched posture; the Menagerie position servos turn those into torques.

Gains were tuned offline by random search on a push benchmark (tests/balance_bench.mjs). Switch the controller off to compare with the servos just holding Menagerie's stand keyframe.

About the policy

The default is diffusion-pick's Diffusion Policy v2 (ResNet18 encoders on the 64×64 front and wrist cameras + proprio, 10-step DDIM, 16-step action chunks), trained with recovery demonstrations (deliberate missed grasps and re-grasps) and strong photometric augmentation. The views above are exactly what it sees. The v1 policy, as exported and fine-tuned on this page's renders, is also in the picker.

The placeholder is a scripted controller compiled to ONNX ops that reads privileged object state instead of images. Any diffusion-pick export loads through Load .onnx… or ?policy=models/<name>.onnx; inputs are matched by name.
